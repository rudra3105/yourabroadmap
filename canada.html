Simplified Visa Process

1

Offer Letter

Apply and receive an acceptance letter from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) in Canada.

2

GIC & Fee Payment

Pay your first-year tuition fees and open a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) of $20,635 CAD for living expenses.

3

Medical & Biometrics

Complete your upfront medical examination and book your biometrics appointment at the VFS Global center.

4

Visa Submission

Submit your Study Permit application via the SDS or Non-SDS category and wait for approval.
